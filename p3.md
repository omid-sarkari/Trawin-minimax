============================================================

First determine their intent.

Create a professional onboarding flow.

Example:

STEP 1 — WHY ARE YOU HERE?

- Find a job
- Get hired by companies
- Evaluate my programming skills
- Improve my programming skills
- Build a verified developer resume
- Participate in competitions
- Prepare for technical interviews
- Learn / practice
- Other

Do not hardcode business logic around only these options.
